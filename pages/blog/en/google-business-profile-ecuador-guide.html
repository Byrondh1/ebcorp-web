Google Business Profile for Businesses in Ecuador: 2026 Guide

When a customer searches for your service on Google Maps, does your company show up? This guide explains how to set up and maintain your free profile, step by step.

A buyer in Ibarra types "welding shop near me" or "electrical engineer in Tulcán" into Google. Three businesses appear on the map, each with a phone number, opening hours and reviews. If yours is not one of them, that customer calls a competitor without ever visiting your website. Your Google Business Profile is the free listing that decides whether you show up at that moment.

What Is Google Business Profile and Why Does It Matter?

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the free listing that shows your company's details on Google Maps and in local search: name, address, phone, hours, photos, reviews and a link to your website. It does not replace your website, it complements it: the profile captures local searches, and the site convinces and converts. If you do not have a site yet, see our web development service.

For B2B companies in northern Ecuador, the profile also works as proof of existence: an industrial supplier with a verifiable address, reviews and photos of finished jobs earns more trust than one with only a WhatsApp number.

How to Create and Verify Your Profile, Step by Step

  1. Search for your business on Google Maps. If a listing already exists, claim it instead of creating a duplicate.
  2. Enter the basics: your real business name, primary category, address or service area, and phone number.
  3. Verify ownership. Depending on the case, Google offers a phone call, text message, email, video or a postcard code. Until you verify, the profile is not fully visible.
  4. Complete the profile: hours, website, services, description and photos.
  5. Check that everything matches your website: same name, address and phone everywhere.
No storefront open to the public? If you work at the customer's site (electrical installation, maintenance, web development), set up a service area instead of showing your address. Google defines the exact requirements and they can change, so check its official help pages first.

How to Optimize Your Google Business Listing

Categories and Services

The primary category is one of the strongest signals for the searches you appear in. Choose the most specific one ("Electrical contractor" rather than "Construction services") and add secondary categories only if they describe real services. Then list each service with a short description.

Photos and Posts

Upload real photos of your team, finished jobs, premises and storefront. Avoid stock images. Regular posts about projects or news show that the business is active.

Reviews: Ask for Them and Answer Them

Reviews weigh heavily on a customer's decision. Ask every satisfied client to leave one by sending the direct link to your profile on WhatsApp right after the job. Reply to all of them, negative ones included, calmly and with facts. Do not buy reviews or offer discounts in exchange: it breaks Google's policies and can get your profile suspended.

Details to Have Ready

  • Your exact trading name, as it appears on your signage and website.
  • A phone number and WhatsApp line you actually answer during business hours.
  • Opening hours, including holidays and appointment-only times.
  • A list of services with a one-sentence description each.
  • At least five original photos: storefront, team and finished jobs.

Profile and Website: How They Work Together

The profile attracts people searching "near me"; the website answers the questions a listing cannot: what your company does, which standards it follows, which projects it has delivered. Link the profile to the page that is most useful to the visitor, not always the homepage, and make sure that page loads well on a phone, where most local searches happen. A complete listing that leads to a slow or messy website wastes the contact you already won.

How to Measure Whether Your Profile Works

Google Business Profile shows how many people viewed your listing, called, asked for directions or visited your site. Review those figures monthly and add tracking parameters to your link so Google Analytics shows how much traffic comes from the profile.
